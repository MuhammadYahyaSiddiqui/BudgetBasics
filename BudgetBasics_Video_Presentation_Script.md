## 🎬 SCENE 7: Module 3 - Advanced Financial Calculators & Formulas (6:00 - 8:30)
**[Screen Action:** Module 3 'Financial Calculators' par scroll karein. Tabs switch karein (Compound Interest, Loan EMI, Emergency Fund, Savings Goal, Debt Payoff, Rule of 72).]**

> **🗣️ Speaking Script:**  
> *"Ab aate hain hamare most powerful section par: **Module 3 - Advanced Financial Calculators Suite**.  
> Yahan humne 6 major real-world financial calculators build kiye hain. Aayein main aapko inke mathematical formulas aur logic simple alfaz mein samjhata hoon:  
>  
> ### 1. Compound Interest Calculator:  
> - **Formula:**  
>   $$A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n \times t}$$  
>   - $P$ = Principal (Shuruati investment)  
>   - $r$ = Annual interest rate  
>   - $n$ = Compounding frequency per year  
>   - $t$ = Time duration in years  
> - **Matlab:** Compound interest mein sirf original raqam par munafa nahi milta, balki jo profit generate hota hai, aglay saal us profit par bhi profit milta hai. Isse graph exponential speed se upar jata hai.  
>  
> ### 2. Loan EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) Calculator:  
> - **Formula:**  
>   $$EMI = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$  
>   - $P$ = Loan Amount, $r$ = Monthly Interest Rate ($\frac{Annual\ Rate}{12 \times 100}$), $n$ = Total months.  
> - **Matlab:** Yeh formula calculate karta hai ke har mahine bank ko kitni exact installment deni hogi, aur isme se kitna paisa asal loan (Principal) ka cut ho raha hai aur kitna bank ka Interest ban raha hai.  
>  
> ### 3. Emergency Fund Target Calculator:  
> - **Formula:**  
>   $$Target = Monthly\ Essential\ Expenses \times Number\ of\ Months\ (3\ to\ 6)$$  
> - **Matlab:** Agar kisi ki job chali jaye ya medical emergency ho jaye, to kam se kam 3 se 6 mahine ke zaroori kharchon ka cash liquid bank account mein hona zaroori hai.  
>  
> ### 4. Savings Goal Timeline Calculator:  
> - **Formula:**  
>   $$Months = \frac{Target\ Amount - Current\ Savings}{Monthly\ Contribution}$$  
> - **Matlab:** Agar aapko gari, ghar ya vacation ke liye paisa jama karna hai to yeh batata hai ke kitne mahino mein goal pura hoga.  
>  
> ### 5. Debt Payoff Strategies (Snowball vs. Avalanche):  
> - **Snowball Method:** Sab se chote karze ko pehle khatam karna (Psychological motivation ke liye).  
> - **Avalanche Method:** Sab se zyada interest rate wale karze ko pehle khatam karna (Maximum paise bachane ke liye).  
>  
> ### 6. Rule of 72 (Doubling Time):  
> - **Formula:**  
>   $$Years \approx \frac{72}{Annual\ Interest\ Rate}$$  
> - **Matlab:** Yeh finance ka famous shortcut rule hai jo batata hai ke kisi bhi fix return par aapka paisa kitne saalon mein double hoga. For example, agar 12% return hai to $\frac{72}{12} = 6$ saal mein aapka paisa 2x ho jayega!"*

# 📐 All Mathematical Formulas & Logic Cheat Sheet (Reference)

Agar video recording ya viva ke doran koi formula specifically explain karna ho to yeh exact reference hai:

| # | Feature / Tool | Mathematical Formula | Simple Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Compound Interest** | $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$ | Asal raqam + pehle saal ke profit par aglay saal mazeed profit milna. |
| **2** | **Loan EMI** | $$EMI = \frac{P \cdot r \cdot (1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$ | Har mahine bank ko di jane wali fixed installment. |
| **3** | **50/30/20 Budgeting** | $$Needs = 50\%, Wants = 30\%, Savings = 20\%$$ | Monthly salary ko 3 standard hisson mein baant kar kharch karna. |
| **4** | **Rule of 72** | $$Years \approx \frac{72}{Interest\ Rate}$$ | Kitne saalon mein aapka paisa double (2x) ho jayega. |
| **5** | **Emergency Fund Target** | $$Target = Monthly\ Expenses \times (3 \text{ to } 6)$$ | Musibat ke waqt ke liye 3 se 6 mahine ka safety net. |

| **7** | **Net Worth** | $$Net\ Worth = Total\ Assets - Total\ Liabilities$$ | Aapke total assets mein se karz nikal kar actual maali haisiyat. |
| **8** | **Debt-to-Income (DTI)** | $$DTI = \left(\frac{Monthly\ Debt\ Payments}{Gross\ Monthly\ Income}\right) \times 100$$ | Income ka kitna percent loan kisto mein ja raha hai (Safe < 36%). |
