## 🎬 SCENE 10: Advanced Financial Calculators & Mathematical Formulas (8:45 - 10:15)
**[Screen Action:** Highlight the financial formulas and calculator tabs (Compound Interest, Loan EMI, Rule of 72, Emergency Fund).]**

> **🗣️ Speaker:**  
> *"Let’s take a look at the **core mathematical formulas** integrated into BudgetBasics:  
>  
> ### 1. Compound Interest Formula:  
> $$A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{n \times t}$$  
> - $P$ is the Principal investment, $r$ is the annual interest rate, $n$ is compounding frequency, and $t$ is time in years.  
> - *Simple Explanation:* You don't just earn profit on your original money; you also earn profit on your accumulated profits, causing your wealth to grow exponentially over time.  
>  
> ### 2. Loan EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) Formula:  
> $$EMI = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}$$  
> - $P$ is Loan Amount, $r$ is Monthly Interest Rate, and $n$ is the loan tenure in months.  
> - *Simple Explanation:* This calculates the exact monthly installment bank payment, breaking down how much goes toward the principal loan and how much goes toward interest.  
>  
> ### 3. Emergency Fund Calculation:  
> $$\text{Target} = \text{Monthly Essential Living Expenses} \times (3 \text{ to } 6)$$  
> - *Simple Explanation:* Protects against unexpected life events or job transitions by reserving 3 to 6 months of living expenses.  
>  
> ### 4. Rule of 72 (Investment Doubling Time):  
> $$\text{Years to Double} \approx \frac{72}{\text{Annual Interest Rate}}$$  
> - *Simple Explanation:* A famous financial mental model. If your investment earns 12% annually, your money doubles in $\frac{72}{12} = 6\text{ years}$!"*

# 📐 Formulas & Algorithmic Reference Cheat Sheet

| # | Tool / Concept | Mathematical Formula | Simple English Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Compound Interest** | $$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$ | Earning interest on both your initial deposit and past accumulated interest. |
| **2** | **Loan EMI** | $$EMI = \frac{P \cdot r \cdot (1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$ | Fixed monthly payment required to pay off a loan over a given duration. |
| **3** | **50/30/20 Rule** | $$50\%\text{ Needs}, 30\%\text{ Wants}, 20\%\text{ Savings}$$ | The gold standard budgeting rule for smart income allocation. |
| **4** | **Rule of 72** | $$\text{Years} \approx \frac{72}{\text{Interest Rate}}$$ | Quick shortcut to find how many years it takes for an investment to double (2x). |
| **5** | **Emergency Cushion** | $$\text{Target} = \text{Monthly Expenses} \times (3 \text{ to } 6)$$ | Safety backup fund to survive 3 to 6 months without income. |
| **6** | **Savings Goal Timeline** | $$\text{Months} = \frac{\text{Target} - \text{Current}}{\text{Monthly Savings}}$$ | Number of months needed to reach a specific financial target. |
| **7** | **Net Worth** | $$\text{Net Worth} = \text{Total Assets} - \text{Total Liabilities}$$ | What you truly own minus what you owe. |
| **8** | **Debt-to-Income (DTI)** | $$\text{DTI} = \left(\frac{\text{Monthly Debt}}{\text{Gross Income}}\right) \times 100$$ | Percentage of your monthly income used to pay off debts (< 36% is safe). |
